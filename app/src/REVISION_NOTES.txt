GyroLock 3.0.42 — Direct Shutter, Direct Zoom, Stabilizer Noise Reduction

Changes:
- Zoom 0.5x / 1x / 2x buttons immediately apply their zoom ratio; no popup menu.
- Shutter sends the still-capture request immediately instead of waiting for a new AF/AE metering cycle.
- Removed pre-capture assist-torch pulse from the shutter path.
- Existing continuous autofocus/exposure remains active. Optional still flash remains controlled by the user's FLASH toggle.
- Increased gyro noise dead-band and stationary-clip tolerance to reduce residual drift while the phone is held still.
- Increased EIS correction gain moderately for stronger compensation during real movement.

Validation:
- ZIP structure/integrity checked.
- Android compilation and Samsung A16 device testing have not yet been performed.
